## 2. Verdict

**libc gate: NOT MET.**

Rows 1 (correctness), 3 (size), 4 (hardening) and 5 (on-target) are NOT MET; rows 2 (portability)
and 6 (documentation) are BLOCKED. MET checks: symbol coverage and the packed symbol list, the 31
host unit-test crates, fuzz replay, the hardened allocator default, the two inherited qtests, page
coverage, the existing docs gate and `libc(7)`. The on-target row is now measured rather than
blocked: `libc-signals` passes; the other six cases fail or block for the reasons in the C74a
matrix below. The gate does not close.

## 4. Retrospective

What is solid: the symbol census and packed-list checks, the 31 host crates, fuzz replay, the
hardened allocator default, the two inherited qtests, and the documentation deliverable. The
man-page coverage is mechanical: 62 candidates are covered by 17 pages and `man-check` is green.

What is missing: four differential families still fail; the size gate has 48 failing rows; the
`inet_text` soak crashed and needs a rerun; the donor suites are blocked on C71a; six of the seven
on-target cases still fail or block (only signals passes); Miri and `mandoc` are absent on this
host; and the ABI-freeze lint finds a mismatch between the frozen map and the C64i exports. None of
these is a documentation failure and none is hidden by the table. The C64i exports themselves are
present: `libc-symbol-audit` reports 66 of 66 required names exported; its gate row is red only
because the C64j expected snapshot still records the pre-C64i MISSING values.

The C27 lesson holds: the gate was evaluated before any claim. The verdict is NOT MET, the two
runs of `eval` agree on the rows (the only churn was the intermittent 120 s `core-servers`
timeout, which passed on the immediate rerun), and the draft amendment below is the NOT MET
variant.
